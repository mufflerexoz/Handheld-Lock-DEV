# Privacy Policy — Handheld Lock

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

Handheld Lock (`com.handheldlock.mufflerexoz`) keeps your phone's screen in landscape
orientation. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## Data collection

Handheld Lock **does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal or sensitive
data**. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device. It
has no ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs.

The only information the app stores is its own settings (whether the lock is on, which
landscape orientation you chose, and your previous auto-rotate setting so it can be restored).
These stay on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Purchases

Handheld Lock Premium is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by **Google Play**.
Handheld Lock never sees or stores your payment details. The app only asks Google Play,
on your device, whether your Google account owns Premium, and stores that yes/no answer
locally. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Accessibility Service

Handheld Lock includes an optional Accessibility Service. It is used **only** to display an
invisible 1-pixel window that requests landscape orientation. Android shows accessibility
windows above the lock screen, which is the only way for an app to rotate the lock screen.

The Accessibility Service:

- does **not** read or retrieve screen content (`canRetrieveWindowContent` is disabled);
- does **not** record keystrokes, text, passwords or any other input;
- does **not** perform actions on your behalf or interact with other apps;
- ignores all accessibility events it receives;
- does **not** collect or share any data.

The app asks for your consent before you are taken to enable it, and you can turn it off at
any time in **Settings → Accessibility**.

## Other permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Modify system settings | Sets the system rotation lock to landscape, and restores it when you turn the lock off. |
| Display over other apps | Shows the invisible orientation window when the Accessibility Service is off. |
| Foreground service / notifications | Keeps the lock running in that fallback mode and shows its status notification. |
| Run at startup | Turns the lock back on after a reboot if you left it on. |

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
